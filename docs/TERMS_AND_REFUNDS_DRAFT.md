## 3. The written scope

After your deposit we send a short written scope listing what is and is not included. Work starts
when you confirm it by replying. Delivery times in the package start from the day you confirm.
If you do not reply to the scope within [14] days, we may close the order and refund the deposit

## 4. Revisions and changes

Each package includes the number of revision rounds it lists. A revision round is one set of
changes you send together. Changes beyond that, or changes that go beyond the confirmed scope, are
quoted before we do them.

## 5. What we need from you

You agree to give us the text, images, logins and decisions we ask for, and to reply within a
reasonable time. If you are late, the delivery time moves by the same amount. You confirm that you
own or have permission to use everything you give us.

## 6. Refunds and cancellations

- Before we send the written scope: you may cancel and receive a full refund of your deposit.
- After you confirm the scope but before work starts: you may cancel and receive your deposit back

- After work has started: the deposit is not refundable, because the time is already spent. If we
  fail to deliver what the scope promises and cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we will refund
  the part of your payment that relates to what we did not deliver.
- After handover: we fix defects in what we delivered for [30] days at no charge. We do not refund
  completed and delivered work.
